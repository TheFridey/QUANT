# Reversible legacy crypto retirement proposal

No scheduler behaviour changed. The pure `assessLegacyRetirement` contract validates the structure of a proposal only and never activates it. Its zero counts do not carry account/environment/snapshot/time identity and cannot establish verified production eligibility. `eligibleForOperatorApproval` means only that these supplied counts pass the structural checks. Current zero-position/order/fill/protection and research-dependency evidence is UNVERIFIED; the earlier reported 103,000 daily observations is not current measurement.

Four independently controlled permissions are required: DISCOVERY_ENABLED, STRATEGY_EVALUATION_ENABLED, NEW_ENTRY_ENABLED and PROTECTIVE_EXIT_ENABLED. Existing legacy behaviour remains the default until an explicitly approved production transition. A gold-only retirement proposal disables the first three and retains protective exits. Missing/malformed obligation counts or unknown research dependencies refuse eligibility for operator approval. A Boolean is not an execution authorisation.

Discovery handles new instruments only. Required price refresh for open positions, pending reductions and protective actions has its own durable watch set and remains available even if discovery is disabled. Strategy-evaluation off skips new signals and agent decision generation, while settlement, reconciliation, protection and archival continue. Entry permission is checked at plan, admit and settle, including queued/retried jobs. Protective exits do not inherit entry spread caps or entry permissions. Cancellation must distinguish pending entries from required reductions.

Before production transition, obtain a consistent account-scoped snapshot with zero open positions, zero pending orders, zero unsettled fills, zero required protective actions and explicit absence of research dependencies on new crypto observations. Under a short operational fence, recheck at transition commit to avoid racing new entries. Drain or reject old queued entry intents; retain all historical rows and financial latches. Record old/new control revisions and actor. Reversal restores scheduler permissions by revision, never resets capital, HWM, daily loss or drawdown.

Estimate only after approved measurements: count observations by source/hour for seven representative days; AgentDecision by agent/cycle; CyclePlan and funnel events; provider request counters; queue arrivals/completions and latency; worker CPU; table and index bytes. Expected observation savings = retired source rows/day minus retained protective-watch rows/day. AgentDecision savings depend on retired cycles times evaluated instruments/agents, not a guaranteed fixed multiple. Storage savings = measured average row+index bytes times avoided rows; existing rows are never deleted by retirement. CPU/provider/queue savings require before/after matched-window telemetry and cannot be inferred from row count alone.

Runtime implementation touches execution-related controls and depends on C10/global stop semantics and independent safety review. It is deliberately not wired into the existing singleton scheduler before those boundaries are approved. See TITANIUM-APPROVAL-PACKAGE D3/C10.
