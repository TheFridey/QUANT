## Accounting audit

`VERIFIED_REPOSITORY`: LONG entry selects ask and exit bid; SHORT entry selects bid and exit ask. P&L is signed price movement divided by explicit point size times GBP stake. Spread is already in the two executable prices and is counted once. Notional is an exposure measure; `marginGbp` computes a reservation rather than deducting notional. Conservative booking floors signed GBP to pennies (positive gains down; losses away from zero). Stake sizing floors to increments, refuses a below-minimum stake, applies risk and margin checks, and P4 reports `cappedAtMaxStake` against the final stake. Ordinary stop gaps return the observed closing-side price at/through the stop, without inventing a stop-price fill.

`VERIFIED_TEST`: bounded sampling checks long/short symmetry, stake grid, stake maximum, available margin and booked estimated loss <= booked permitted risk. Existing flat-price round-trip and gap tests remain. Funding/actual partial-fill settlement are not implemented and are not asserted as complete. Ordinary stop loss estimates are explicitly not maximum losses.

No evidence in the committed branch diff establishes when or why an uncommitted `1e400` assertion was removed; the defect itself is independently reproduced. The removed-test claim remains `UNVERIFIED` historical attribution.
